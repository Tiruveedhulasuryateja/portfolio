Engineering software that defends nations

I'm a Software Engineer with 2+ years of experience designing and building desktop applications, automated test equipment (ATE), and instrument-control software for India's aerospace and defence programmes. My work spans the full lifecycle — requirement analysis, GUI development, hardware–software integration, verification, and technical documentation.
